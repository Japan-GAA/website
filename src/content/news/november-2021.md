## **Roll On 2022**

Usually gearing up for the AGGs at this time of the year, but hopefully come next year things will be back to normal. Not much going on as a result so it’s a short newsletter this month…

例年だと、AGGに向け練習に熱が入る時期ですが、残念ながら今年は大きな大会がありません。来年には色々なことが通常に戻ることを祈っています。今月も短いニュースレターです…

![](/news/16366935-IMG_4500.webp)

## **Japan GAA Wolfhounds**

![](/news/16366932-IMG_4415.webp)

  After a very much improved performance last Sunday night, the Wolfhounds lost out by less than a goal to the Senshu Powers.

After leading the whole game we conceded three goals in the last quarter to just come up short.

The lads next game will be in the Quarter Finals in few weeks time…

日曜晩の試合は、以前よりもパフォーマンスが良く、大健闘でしたが、惜しくも専修パワーズに勝利ならず。

全体的にリードするシーンの多い内容でしたが、最後のクオーターで、3ゴールを許してしまいました。

次は数週間後に準々決勝があります…!

First up, is Naoko, one of our longest club members! She’s been playing with the team for five years. The photo is with her princess, Marine-chan!

まず最初は尚子さん！クラブ歴５年！お写真は娘様の麻倫ちゃんと！

![](/news/16366937-IMG_4498.webp)

  Local Club / Hometown: Japan GAA / Shinjuku

How long have you been playing GAA: 5 years

Favourite sportsperson: Ichiro

Favorite Sport/hobby outside GAA: Rugby, Soccer, Pole Dancing

Favorite movie: Mission Impossible, The Notebook
