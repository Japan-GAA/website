Family-friendly event, Sports Day is coming up at Komazawa Olympic Park! where you can experience many kind of sports, like Hurling, Gaelic football, Tag rugby, Rapid ball etc. No admission fee needed, everyone is welcome! There will be Gaelic football “Kids session” from 9am which is super fun and great to start out the day! Come join us to our borderless sports event. Please see below for time table.
Interesed in Irish beverages?  The “Bar Japan GAA”  will be open through out the day, you will have hands full ;) Don’t miss it!

At reception, you will need to provide your name to enter the facility, please fill out form below prior to the event.
