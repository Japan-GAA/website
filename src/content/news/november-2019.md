**4 Days until KL!**

![](/news/15831277-IMG_6677.webp)

  Not going to repeat anything as everybody knows everything that is needed by now... Just a few key things to remember though

- Gum shields are compulsory

- Travel insurance is mandatory

- Costume for Sunday night

- Travel adapters

- Bring white socks and the shorts that were handed out at training

- Buses will leave from the hotel on both Saturday and Sunday morning between 6.30am and 7.00am

**AGGs Itinerary**

This is a rough itinerary for those who are attending their first AGGs, might be useful to know...

Friday 8th Nov - AGG Club Day @ Hotel Istana - 2.00pm to 6.00pm

Friday 8th Nov - Opening ceremony @ Hotel Istana - 7.30pm to 10.00pm

Saturday 9th Nov - Group Games for mens and ladies - 7.30am to 6.00pm

Saturday 9th Nov - Team Dinner @ Drift Dining - 8.00pm

Sunday 10th Nov - Final Group Games and Finals - 7.30am to 6.00pm

Sunday 10th Nov - Awards Dinner @ Hotel Istana Ballroom - 8.00pm to 12.30am
