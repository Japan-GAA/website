暑さに負けず、この夏も全力で練習に打ち込みました！８月には長野での強化合宿も！秋のAGGに向けて引き続き頑張りましょう！
We gave it our all in training this summer, not letting the heat get the best of us! In August, we also had an intensive training camp in Nagano. Let’s keep up the hard work as we head toward AGG!

**ボーリング大会 / Bowling night**
Japan GAAボーリング大会開催！普段では見られないみんなの秘めた実力が大爆発！
母国へ旅立つ仲間との最後の思い出にもなりました。その後続くはカラオケ大会。夏の良い思い出となりました！

Everyone’s hidden talents were on full display in a way we don’t usually get to see! It also became a memorable farewell event for our teammate heading off to their country. The fun continued with a karaoke afterwards — a great way to wrap up a summer full of memories!

![](/news/3c6c7351-IMG_9690.webp)

  **長野強化合宿バーベキュー / Training & BBQ in Nagano**

緑に囲まれたフィールドで、思いっきり練習しました。夕方にはBBQで盛り上がり、夜通し語らい賑やかな２泊３日となりました。最後は美味しいそばを食べて締めくくり！

We practiced wholeheartedly in a green, open field. In the evening, we had a lively BBQ, and spent the night chatting away, making it a fun and bustling three-days. We wrapped it up by enjoying some delicious soba noodles!
